I'm Sorry lovey

I'm really sorry, love. I've been thinking a lot about how I've been treating you lately, and honestly, I know I haven't been the same person you first met.

At the beginning, I was so sweet to you. I always made you feel loved, appreciated, and treasured. I made you feel like you were someone really important to me, and I know I gave you the feeling that I would always make an effort for you. But lately, I feel like I've slowly stopped being that person.

I'm sorry because there are times when you're hurt or upset with me and instead of trying my best to understand you and fix things, I just leave it alone or act like it isn't a big deal. I know that probably makes you feel like your feelings don't matter to me anymore.

And honestly, you don't deserve that from me.

I realize that i've become the kind of boyfriend I promised myself I wouldn't become. I used to care so much about the little things. I used to make sure you felt loved, and I used to make an effort to make you feel special.

Now, whenever we have an argument, sometimes I just let it pass without properly fixing it. I know saying "sorry" after everything isn't enough, especially when my actions keep making you feel the same way.

"I know that words mean nothing if my actions don't change."

I'm sorry if I made you feel like I don't treasure you anymore. I'm sorry if I made you question whether you're still important to me. I'm sorry if the way I act now makes you miss the version of me who used to give you so much attention and reassurance.

I know I can't just tell you that I still care and expect everything to be okay. I understand that my actions are what really matter.

I don't want to blame you, our arguments, my mood, being tired, games, or anything else for the way I've been treating you. Those things might explain why I acted a certain way, but they don't excuse it.

I should have communicated better. I should have listened instead of ignoring things. I should have tried to understand you instead of letting the problem sit there until it became even bigger.

I'm sorry for every time you had to wonder, "Does he even care anymore?" when I should have been making you feel secure instead.

You shouldn't have to beg for the same effort and affection that I willingly gave you before.

I also want to apologize for the times I made you feel alone even though you were supposed to have me beside you. I know being in a relationship isn't just about being there when everything is okay.

It's also about choosing to communicate when things get difficult, listening when the other person is hurt, and actually trying to fix things instead of avoiding them.

I know I can't change everything that already happened. I can't take back the moments where I ignored you, disappointed you, or made you feel less loved than you deserved.

But I can acknowledge what I did wrong and actually work on changing it.

I don't want to bring back the old version of me just through words. I want to show you through my actions that I still care.

I want to learn how to communicate with you better, how to handle our arguments more maturely, and how to stop taking your presence for granted.

If you're hurt, I want to learn to listen instead of becoming distant. If we have a problem, I want to work through it instead of pretending it doesn't exist.

I'm not asking you to immediately forget everything or forgive me just because I said sorry. I understand if you're still hurt or disappointed. You have every right to feel that way.

I just want you to know that I recognize my mistakes, and I genuinely regret making you feel like you're no longer treasured by me.

You were someone I once made feel so special, and it hurts me to realize that lately, I've been making you feel the opposite.

You deserve consistency, effort, patience, reassurance, and genuine care not just during the good days, but especially during the difficult ones.

I'm sorry for becoming distant. I'm sorry for the arguments I failed to properly fix. I'm sorry for the times I chose to ignore things instead of understanding you.

And most of all, I'm sorry if I made you feel like my love and effort disappeared.

I still care about you deeply. I just know that caring isn't enough if I don't know how to show it properly. So I want to do better — not because I'm scared of losing you, but because you deserve a boyfriend who actually makes an effort to treat you right.

I'm really sorry for everything, love. I know I have a lot to improve, and I don't expect you to believe me immediately. I'll let my actions speak for me.

I just hope that, little by little, I can make you feel treasured again not because I'm trying to recreate the beginning of our relationship, but because I want to become a better partner for you moving forward.
